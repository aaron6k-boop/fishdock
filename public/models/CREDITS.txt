FishDock's 3D equipment models (reel.glb, nets.glb) were modelled in Blender for FishDock (source-assets/blender/).

The net mesh textures are from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), by Lennart Demes, CC0 1.0 (public domain):
  Net001A (drop net), Net002A (landing net).
No attribution is required by the licence; credited here with thanks.
The full list of third-party assets, with sources, licences and file hashes: source-assets/ASSET-LICENCES.json.

fp_arms.glb (your forearms and hands in first person) is cut from a person made with MPFB, MakeHuman for Blender
(https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/), with the MakeHuman system assets' skin; the people MPFB makes and the
system assets are CC0 1.0. Credited with thanks to the MakeHuman team.

pierprops.glb (the pier's lamps, lifebuoy stations, bollards and benches): the bollard, bench and lifebuoy housing were
modelled in Blender for FishDock; the lamp is Poly Haven's "Street Lamp 01" and the buoy Poly Haven's "Lifebuoy"
(https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0. The pier deck's concrete (public/textures/pier_concrete_*.jpg) is Poly Haven's
"Concrete Floor 02", CC0 1.0. Credited with thanks to Poly Haven and its artists.

The pier's other photographed surfaces (public/textures/, cut down to 512 or 1024 px by
source-assets/prepare_textures.py) are also from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0:
  deck_worn_diff.jpg: "Concrete Floor Worn 001" by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers (worn patches on the deck);
  wall_rust_*.jpg: "Rusty Painted Metal" by Amal Kumar (rust runs and pitting on the harbour wall's steel piling);
  wall_moss_diff.jpg: "Concrete Moss" by Rob Tuytel (the weed on the wall round the waterline);
  cap_concrete_*.jpg: "Concrete Wall 004" by Dario Barresi and Charlotte Baglioni (the wall's concrete cap);
  brick_red_*.jpg: "Red Brick 03" from Poly Haven (the coastwatch building's brick);
  plaster_*.jpg: "Painted Plaster Wall" from Poly Haven (the coastwatch building's white paint).
  ground_rough_*.jpg: "Coast Sand Rocks 02" from Poly Haven (the cliff's face and the dune).
  rock_granite_*.jpg: made for FishDock from noise (source-assets/make_granite.py), no outside image.
The weathering on the railings, lamp columns, furniture, timber, drains and stains is drawn on the phone
(src/lib/world3d/textures3d.js); no third-party assets.

fish.glb (cod, bass, whiting, mackerel, plaice and pollack bodies, fins and eyes) was modelled in Blender for FishDock
from the outlines of FishDock's own species pictures (source-assets/blender/build_fish.py); the pictures are laid
over it in the app.

rodkit.glb (the sea-spinning rod: its handle, reel seat, guides, whippings, tip ring and blank, and the spinning reel with
its rotor, bail, line roller, spool and handle) was modelled in Blender for FishDock (source-assets/blender/
build_rodkit.py); no third-party assets. The game bends the blank and turns the reel.
