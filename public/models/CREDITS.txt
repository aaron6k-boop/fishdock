FishDock's 3D equipment models (reel.glb, nets.glb) were modelled in Blender for FishDock (source-assets/blender/).

The net mesh textures are from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), by Lennart Demes, CC0 1.0 (public domain):
  Net001A (drop net), Net002A (landing net).
No attribution is required by the licence; credited here with thanks.
The full list of third-party assets, with sources, licences and file hashes: source-assets/ASSET-LICENCES.json.

fp_rig.glb (your arms, hands and jacket in first person, with their skeleton and clips) is the realistic male body in
Blender Studio's Human Base Meshes bundle, version 1.1.0 (https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/; CC0 1.0 as
announced at its release, the bundle itself carries no licence file), rigged with Rigify in Blender for FishDock, its
hands and forearms cut from the body and its jacket made from the body (source-assets/blender/rig/). Its grips, the
reel's ten turns and the cast were posed and solved in Blender on that rig. The body's turn through the cast comes from
the "Fishing Cast" motion capture on Adobe Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com), which Adobe allows royalty free in games;
only that turn, carried onto the rig, is in the file. Credited with thanks to Blender Studio and Adobe.

pierprops.glb (the pier's lamps, lifebuoy stations, bollards and benches): the bollard, bench and lifebuoy housing were
modelled in Blender for FishDock (source-assets/blender/props2/build_pierprops2.py, from 18 November 2026); the lamp is
Poly Haven's "Street Lamp 01" and the buoy Poly Haven's "Lifebuoy" (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0. The bench's slats
are textured with Poly Haven's "Brown Planks 03" (CC0 1.0), cut to four of its boards. The lifebuoy station's name board
and 999 notice were drawn for FishDock, lettered in Inter (by Rasmus Andersson, SIL Open Font License 1.1). The pier deck's concrete (public/textures/deck_broom_*.jpg, from 17 November 2026)
is Poly Haven's "Concrete Floor 03" (broom-finished, by Rob Tuytel and Matterfield), CC0 1.0; before it, "Concrete Floor
02" (public/textures/pier_concrete_*.jpg). Credited with thanks to Poly Haven and its artists.

armour.glb (the rock armour's stones, from 17 November 2026): four scanned boulders from Poly Haven
(https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0: "Boulder 01" by Rico Cilliers, "Namaqualand Boulder 03" and "Namaqualand Boulder 05"
by Jenelle van Heerden and Dario Barresi, and "Namaqualand Boulder 04" by Jenelle van Heerden. Turned down to fewer
triangles and their photographs tinted towards a grey granite by source-assets/blender/build_armour.py. Credited with
thanks to Poly Haven and its artists.

(src/lib/world3d/textures3d.js); no third-party assets.

fish.glb (cod, bass, whiting, mackerel, plaice and pollack bodies, fins and eyes) was modelled in Blender for FishDock
from the outlines of FishDock's own species pictures (source-assets/blender/build_fish.py); the pictures are laid
over it in the app.

rodkit.glb (the sea-spinning rod: its handle, reel seat, guides, whippings, tip ring and blank, and the spinning reel with
its rotor, bail, line roller, spool and handle) was modelled in Blender for FishDock (source-assets/blender/
build_rodkit.py); no third-party assets. The game bends the blank and turns the reel.

The recorded sounds (public/sounds/: the sea against the pier, water lapping, the harbour and its gulls, wind, rain on the
deck and in puddles, a fishing reel turned slow, steady and fast, and splashes) are from BigSoundBank
(https://bigsoundbank.com), recorded by Joseph Sardin and Denis Chardonnet, CC0 1.0 (public domain): no attribution is
required; credited here with thanks. Cut to loops and levelled by source-assets/prepare_sounds.py; the list of
recordings, with their pages and file hashes, is in source-assets/ASSET-LICENCES.json.
Added 17 November 2026, from the same library and under the same licence: the cast's whoosh (a long stick, a metal
tube and a rope swung through the air), single clicks of a small and a large ratchet for the reel's drag, and a rope
run through the hands for the drop net, and a shoe's steps on concrete (cut by source-assets/prepare_sounds2.py).
And later the same day, from the same library and licence, all by Joseph Sardin: three claps of thunder, near to far,
one strike of a small bell for the rod-tip bell, drops of water and water swirling for the net draining and going in,
and slaps (made duller) for a fish's tail on the mat (cut by source-assets/prepare_sounds3.py).
