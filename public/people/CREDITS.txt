FishDock: the people in the 3D view

The people (gardener.glb, m03.glb, m04.glb, m05.glb, m10.glb, m12.glb, m17.glb, m18.glb, f04.glb, f07.glb, f09.glb,
f14.glb) and their motion-captured walking, standing and looking-round movements (anims.glb) are from the Microsoft
Rocketbox avatar library (https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), converted to glTF for the web: textures
reduced in size, the facial animation and finger detail left out, the walks taken out of their own forward travel.
The *_hd.glb files (loaded on the High quality and Ultra settings) are the same people with the library's own
source textures at full size: colour maps at 2048 px and normal maps at 1024 px (source-assets/people_hd.py).

The hats, hood, life jacket and wellies drawn over them, and the clothing colours, are FishDock's own.

anims2.glb: kneeling, kneeling to look closely, crouching and a fishing stance, from Adobe Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com;
Fishing Idle, Kneeling Idle, Kneeling Inspecting, Crouching Idle), retargeted in Blender from Mixamo's Y Bot rig onto
the Rocketbox skeleton (source-assets/blender/retarget_mixamo.py). Mixamo's animations may be used royalty-free in
personal, commercial and non-profit projects under Adobe's terms; the files themselves aren't redistributed as such.

angler1.glb (and angler1_hd.glb): the man with short hair (m05.glb, Microsoft Rocketbox, MIT License, as above) with a
fishing jacket, trousers and wellies modelled in Blender from his own body (source-assets/blender/make_angler_clothes.py),
skinned to the same skeleton. The cloth's surface detail is from Poly Haven's fabric_pattern_07 and denim_fabric (CC0).
