# FishDock against other fishing games (15 November 2026)

What other fishing games do to keep it interesting, what FishDock already has, and what fits its realistic, British,
on-the-pier identity. Sources are the games' own store pages and press notes, read on 15 November 2026: only what
those pages say is listed.

| What they do | Who (source) | FishDock | Fits? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each species behaves differently by time of day, place and what you do | Call of the Wild: The Angler ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1408610/)); Russian Fishing 4 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Fishing_4)) | Bites follow the real weather, tide and light at the place | Yes: more per-species timing where sources support it (backlog item 9) |
| Many species to collect, harder "legendary" fish | The Angler; RF4 (110 species) | Species book, medals to Specimen, personal bests | Specimens yes; legendary fish no (not real) |
| Earn and progress: money, gear, new places | RF4 (cafe quests, gear and places bought) | Levels, XP, badges, three daily challenges | Gear progression maybe later; never paying to fish real places |
| Fish together, up to 12 friends | The Angler | Fish Together parties, public places (four a room) | Yes |
| Text chat with friends and strangers | The Angler (update 1.2.7, [press note](https://news.cision.com/avalanche-studios/r/game-update-1-2-7-brings-new-content--multiplayer-features--and-qol-improvements-to-call-of-the-wild,c3749581)) | Typed chat in parties; set phrases in public places (PR "Chat") | Yes, phrases only with strangers |
| Competitions | The Angler ("compete") | Party challenges; **the hourly match in public places (this PR)** | Yes: scored the catch-and-release shore way |
| Several ways to fish: float, lure, bottom | The Angler | Bottom fishing from the pier | Float and lure from the pier later |
| Boats and bait boats | The Angler (paid packs) | None | Not for a pier game |

Next candidates, in order: a weekly board per place (best match total, biggest fish), float and lure fishing from the
pier, and per-species bite times where the sources support them.
